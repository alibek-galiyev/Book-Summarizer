# Task: Take faithful story notes on one chunk of a chapter

Chapter title: {title}
Chunk: {chunk_index} of {chunk_count}

Take notes on **only the text below**. A later step turns the notes into the chapter summary, so favor accurate detail over polished prose. If the text starts or ends mid-scene, note that; do not guess how the scene began or ends.

Aim for 300–700 words: fewer if the text is short, more if it is eventful. Return Markdown under these headings; where the text has nothing for a heading, write "Not specified."

## Events in order
- What happens, scene by scene, in the order the text presents it: decisions, discoveries, conflicts, and reveals, with who does what to whom.
- Mark flashbacks, dreams, letters, and shifts of viewpoint as such.

## Characters
- For each character who appears or is discussed: what they do, want, feel, or learn here, and any change in their relationships.

## Setting and atmosphere
- Where and when the scenes take place, the mood, and world-building details (rules, places, history, objects) introduced here.

## Themes, motifs, and notable lines
- Recurring images, symbols, or ideas, each tied to the moment where it appears.
- At most three short quotes that carry weight, copied exactly from the text.
- The narrative voice: point of view, tense, tone.

## Continuity notes
- Questions raised, threats or promises left hanging, and anything that looks like foreshadowing.
- Scenes that are cut off at the start or end of this chunk, and passages that are unreadable.
