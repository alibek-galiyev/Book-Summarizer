# Task: Write the chapter summary

Chapter title: {title}

Below are notes on this chapter, one set per chunk, in chapter order. Turn them into **one** integrated summary, not a summary per chunk.

## Rules
- Use only the notes. If two notes conflict, keep both versions and say so briefly.
- Merge repeated material: explain each idea once.
- A later chunk may finish an argument or answer a question that an earlier one left open; report as unresolved only what is still open at the end.
- Preserve the chapter's line of reasoning and the author's distinct position; keep evidence, hypothesis, interpretation, and speculation apart.
- Explain mechanisms and why each major claim matters. Keep the most informative examples, not every anecdote.
- Aim for 400–700 words, fewer for a short chapter; **never exceed 1,000 words**. To save space, drop secondary items and repetition before qualifications or causal links.
- Use exactly the seven sections below, with these headings and numbers. If the notes have nothing for a section, write "Not specified." under it rather than padding it.
- Output only the summary, as Markdown without a code fence.

## Output structure

# {title}

## 1. Chapter Overview
One concise paragraph: the core question, central argument, and contribution of this chapter. Do not presume knowledge of the rest of the book.

## 2. Key Ideas and Concepts
Up to 7 important ideas. For each, a `### Concept name` heading, then its meaning, the author's reasoning, and implications.

## 3. Evidence and Examples
The most revealing facts, findings, historical cases, or thought experiments, and what each demonstrates. Distinguish correlation from causation where the notes do.

## 4. Author's Perspective and Arguments
Distinctive assumptions, argumentative strategies, or disagreements with others. Do not repeat the central argument.

## 5. Critical Insights and Limitations
Limitations, counterarguments, or uncertainty found in the notes. Do not add outside critiques.

## 6. Practical Applications
Actionable implications that follow from the notes. Do not force advice onto theoretical material.

## 7. Key Takeaways
Up to 5 concise points for recall, without repeating whole explanations.
